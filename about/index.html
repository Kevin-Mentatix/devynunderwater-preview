About

The diver behind the lens

What started as a way to remember dives became the reason for them. I shoot macro — the tiny, strange, spectacular life that hides on every reef and under every pier piling. My home water is Blue Heron Bridge in Florida, one of the best small-critter dive sites anywhere, and my logbook keeps pulling me back to the Caribbean: Cozumel's walls, Roatán's reefs, and wherever the next small thing is hiding.

Macro photography rewards patience more than luck: hovering still enough, long enough, for a shrimp to go back to work or a frogfish to forget you exist. That's the part I love — the ocean carries on like you're not there, and every so often it lets you keep a frame of it.

Everything on this site was shot by me, on dives I logged. If you want to know the story behind a photo — or the dive site — ask.
